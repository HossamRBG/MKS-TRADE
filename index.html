About Us

MKS TRADE LLC has grown into one of the largest processors and marketers of ferrous & non-ferrous scrap metal and a leading supplier of prime and secondary steel products by providing the highest level of customer service, leveraging its marketing expertise to offer competitive pricing and being creative in finding solutions for its customers’ needs.

How we can help you?

MKS market teams provide fast and professional customer service having competency within technical and commercial areas. We secure primary aluminum for remelting on the global market and we can supply you with primary aluminum in various shapes and qualities worldwide. Our product portfolio ranges from standard ingots, T-bars or low-profile sows. Our regional market teams, who have market-related competencies, provide you with fast and professional service.

Our Mission in 2010

At MKS TRADE LLC, we are committed to delivering high-quality ferrous and non-ferrous metals, primary aluminum, and steel products to global markets. Through our extensive network spanning over 120 countries and 20 years of expertise, we provide safe, efficient, and competitive trading solutions. With a focus on customer satisfaction, sustainability, and continuous growth, we empower businesses by facilitating seamless international trade and logistics.

Our Vision in 2010

To be the global leader in the trade and processing of ferrous and non-ferrous metals, providing innovative solutions and unmatched customer service while fostering sustainable and profitable growth for our partners worldwide.

Our Mission in 2020

MKS TRADE LLC strives to be the trusted partner in the global metal and steel industry by leveraging our extensive network, market expertise, and commitment to quality. Our Vision in 2020
